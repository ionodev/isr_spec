What the model includes, and its limits
  • Electrons and up to two ion species, Maxwellian, the same ion temperature for both.
  • No collisions, as in isr_spec. Within about 1° of perpendicular to B, Coulomb collisions control the spectrum (Milla & Kudeki 2011), so the spectrum shown there is not physical.
  • The line-of-sight velocity moves the whole plasma: it enters as the Doppler-shifted frequency ω − k·v of every species (K&M eq. 40), which shifts the spectrum by −2vf/c. Positive v is away from the radar.
  • Total power is the integral over the frequency span shown; widen the span to include all of the ion line, or the plasma lines.
  • Numerics: Gordeyev integrals by a trapezoid rule with end corrections, for all frequencies at once by a chirp-z transform. Agrees with the Python isr_spec to about 1e-9 of the peak (see validation/).
